Built so the wrong person can’t open the wrong record

  • Short-lived sessions

    Sign-in tokens last 15 minutes and are never written to browser storage. A secure cookie keeps you signed in for up to 7 days.

  • Stolen sessions end themselves

    If an old session token is ever reused, every session for that account is closed at once.

  • Brute force gets nowhere

    Five wrong passwords lock the account for 15 minutes, and each network address is rate limited.

  • Sign in your way

    Use your email or username with a password, or continue with Google. A forgotten password is reset with a one-time link sent by email.

  • Every sensitive change is logged

    Salary, status and role changes are recorded with who made them, when, and from where.

Your team’s records are one sign-in away.
